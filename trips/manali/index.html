Mountains, at your own pace.

Paragliding over Solang, the long road through the Atal Tunnel, and cafés where nobody checks the time.

Solang Valley, Manali

Manali is where a lot of people take their first real trip with friends. Seven days, a group, and a valley that has something for every kind of brave.

Day by day

  1. Day 1 · Manali

    Hello, mountains

    Arrive in Manali by overnight bus. Check in, rest, and walk Mall Road in the evening.

  2. Day 2 · Solang

    Into the sky

    Solang Valley for paragliding, the ropeway and snow play in season.

  3. Day 3 · Atal Tunnel

    Through the mountain

    Drive through the Atal Tunnel to Sissu and the Lahaul valley on the other side.

  4. Day 4 · Old Manali

    Temples and hot springs

    Hadimba Temple, Vashisht hot springs, and the cafés of Old Manali.

  5. Day 5 · Kullu

    River day

    Rafting on the Beas and a stop at the Kullu shawl weavers.

  6. Day 6 · Naggar

    Castle views

    Naggar Castle and the Roerich art gallery, or a free day to explore.

  7. Day 7 · Home

    Back to Rourkela

    Overnight bus out of the mountains and on your way home.

Day plans can change with weather, roads and permits. We confirm the final plan on WhatsApp before you pay.
