LINE G4 · GOA

Sunsets are better together.

Old churches, spice farms, jet skis, a river cruise at sunset, and the slow ride back from South Goa with the windows down.

Ask for dates on WhatsApp

Goa is more than a party town. We plan it as a beach holiday with real Goan food, old churches and quiet South Goa beaches, so it works for friends, couples and families.

The route · 5 stops

The plan

  1. STOP 1 · NORTH GOA

    Feet in the sand

    Check in near the beach and spend the evening on Calangute or Baga.

  2. STOP 2 · NORTH GOA

    Forts and water sports

    Fort Aguada, Chapora Fort, and water sports like jet skis and banana rides.

  3. STOP 3 · OLD GOA

    Churches and temples

    Basilica of Bom Jesus, Se Cathedral, and Mangueshi Temple, then a spice farm lunch.

  4. STOP 4 · SOUTH GOA

    The quiet coast

    Colva and Palolem beaches, and a sunset cruise on the Mandovi river.

  5. STOP 5 · HOME

    Back to Rourkela

    Breakfast by the sea, then your journey home.

Day plans can change with weather, roads and permits. We confirm the final plan on WhatsApp before you pay.
